Android App Guide

This is a basic guide to the best Android Apps to store your Boarding Passes. Most of the apps
shown here are 3rd party, and may not always be 100% accurate for live time flight information.

The apps can be revised and updated at short notice, so the information shown here is subject
to change.

You should also be aware that there are other factors that can cause trouble. This can happen
when the flight data is not found in the app's database. Examples of this include:

* New airlines
* New routes
* Changes to schedules and flight numbers
* Small remote airports
* Codeshares
* Multi-leg flights using the same flight number
* Private charters

-----------------------------------------
--- Airline's Own App ---

Pros:
+ Most accurate for live time information on Arrivals, Departures, Terminals, Gates, and Delays

Cons:
- You will need to download an app for each airline you use
- Cannot be used to test demonstration boarding passes

-----------------------------------------
--- KDE Itinerary ---

Pros:
+ Open Source, and totally free to use
+ Easy to share and transfer data between devices via .itinerary files
+ Can also be used to plan trips

Cons:
- Can be slightly difficult to use for first time users
- There is no live time flight information (Terminals, Gates, Delays Etc ...)

-----------------------------------------
--- Google Wallet ---

Pros:
+ Very popular and widely available
+ Integration with other Google services
+ Can be used for other purposes

Cons:
- You will need to sign up to Google to use the app
- Reports of Google's Gemini AI causing some problems to some passes being created

-----------------------------------------
--- PassAndroid ---

Pros:
+ Open Source, and totally free to use
+ Data can be exported and shared with other PassAndroid users using open source .espass files
+ Can be used for other purposes

Cons:
- Designs are very basic
- There is no live time flight information (Terminals, Gates, Delays Etc ...)
- Details about the flight must be entered manually
- Reports from some users saying PassAndroid is not in active development

-----------------------------------------
--- Passmint ---

Pros:
+ Open Source, and totally free to use
+ Can be customized, and used for other purposes
+ Data can be exported and shared with other Passmint users
+ Allows passes to be imported into Google Wallet

Cons:
- There is no live time flight information (Terminals, Gates, Delays Etc ...)
- A few details about the flight must be entered manually

-----------------------------------------
--- FossWallet ---

Pros:
+ Open Source, and totally free to use
+ Can be used for other purposes

Cons:
- There is no live time flight information (Terminals, Gates, Delays Etc ...)

-----------------------------------------
--- PassMate ---

Pros:
+ One of the best apps to import and display .pkpass files
+ Totally free with no ads

Cons:
- The app only imports and displays .pkpass files, and cannot be used for anything else

-----------------------------------------
--- Flight Boarding Pass Wallet ---

Pros:
+ Attractive design
+ Can show boarding pass in AZTEC, PDF417, and QR
+ Can show details of more than one leg of a flight
+ Major improvements with an upgrade to v3 in October 2026

Cons:
- Proprietary, and the free version has ads
- Live time flight information availability is not always 100%, always check the latest up to date informations at the airport

-----------------------------------------
--- Flight Status + Boarding Pass ---

Pros:
+ Quick and easy to use

Cons:
- Cannot import barcode from images (.png .jpg Etc ...)
- If the flight does not exist in the app's database the pass gets rejected
- Reports of inaccurate information and details for some flights

-----------------------------------------
--- MyFlights ---

Pros:
+ Quick and easy to use

Cons:
- You will need an account to use the app
- Not 100% free, reports of paid subscription required for live time flight information

-----------------------------------------
--- Fobi PassWallet ---

Pros:
+ Quick and easy to use
+ Can be used for other purposes

Cons:
- The boarding pass has a yellow design background and this cannot be changed
- There is no live time flight information (Terminals, Gates, Delays Etc ...)

-----------------------------------------
--- Technofy Infotech Flight Boarding Pass ---

Pros:
+ Can show boarding pass in AZTEC, PDF417, and QR
+ Has a World Clock Converter

Cons:
- Proprietary, and the app has ads
- There is no live time flight information (Terminals, Gates, Delays Etc ...)

-----------------------------------------
--- Pass2U ---

Pros:
+ Can be customized using templates, and used for other purposes

Cons:
- Proprietary, has ads, and is not 100% free
- There is no live time flight information (Terminals, Gates, Delays Etc ...)
- Details about the flight must be entered manually

-----------------------------------------
--- Aviate: Flight Companion ---

Pros:
+ Can show boarding pass in AZTEC and QR
+ Has various security and extra features

Cons:
- You will need an account to use the app
- Proprietary, and Not 100% free
- Screenshots are disabled

-----------------------------------------
--- FlightElite ---

Pros:
+ Has a large number of features

Cons:
- Not 100% free, reports of paid subscription required to scan boarding passes
